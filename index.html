Core Capabilities

What Nova Fresh Does

Nova Fresh International provides end-to-end commercial, sourcing, quality, and coordination capabilities across four foundational pillars of international food trade.

Market Development

  • Identify and develop new international markets
  • Help suppliers enter or expand in specific target markets
  • Identify potential buyers and commercial opportunities
  • Develop products and programs around market requirements

Sourcing & Supplier Connections

  • Identify suitable producers and exporters
  • Match buyer requirements with appropriate suppliers
  • Coordinate specifications, pricing and commercial discussions
  • Help buyers access multiple potential sources

Inspection & Quality Support

  • Pre-shipment / loading inspections at source
  • Arrival quality inspections at destination ports
  • Product specification verification
  • Quality issue and claim support

Trade & Market Support

  • Market intelligence & crop trend reports
  • Commercial negotiations & program planning
  • Export/import coordination & trade compliance
  • Cold-chain, logistics & phytosanitary coordination
