Core Expertise

What We Do

We focus on three primary services to support produce growers, exporters, and importers.

1. Market Development

We help growers and exporters turn new markets into real business opportunities by identifying qualified buyers, understanding market requirements and developing the relationships needed to establish sustainable sales.

2. International Produce Sourcing

We help importers find the right products and reliable suppliers by leveraging our market relationships and on-the-ground knowledge—saving time, reducing sourcing risk and helping buyers access opportunities they may not find on their own.

3. Independent Produce Inspection & Quality Verification

We provide independent, on-the-ground produce inspection at critical points in the supply chain—from source inspections for importers to receiving and unloading inspections in Japan for growers and exporters. Our role is to document actual product condition, identify quality issues early and provide an independent set of eyes that helps protect both the product and the business relationship.
