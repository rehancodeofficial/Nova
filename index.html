Commercial Market Solutions
Marketplace Structure

Commercial Trade Integration for Producers & Buyers

Nova Fresh International facilitates direct alignment between agricultural growers seeking export expansion and commercial importers requiring dependable produce streams.

Supply Side

For Producers & Exporters

Establishing direct commercial access to premium import markets and structuring long-term supplier agreements.

  • Market Access & Expansion Entry strategy and ongoing commercial distribution across key import hubs in Japan, South Korea, and North America.
  • Buyer Qualification Direct introductions to vetted receivers, wholesalers, and retail distributors with verified credit standing.
  • Export Protocol Compliance Guidance on country-specific phytosanitary rules, maximum residue limits (MRLs), and packaging standards.
  • Commercial Program Development Structuring multi-week seasonal supply commitments to balance harvest volume and optimize grower returns.
Demand Side

For Importers & Buyers

Securing high-quality fresh produce supplies through direct origin sourcing and transparent trade coordination.

  • Direct Producer Introductions Establishing transparent working relationships with certified growers and packers in key production regions.
  • Seasonal Supply Planning Coordinating crop forecasts, volume estimates, and arrival schedules ahead of key retail sales windows.
  • Arrival Quality & Claim Support Independent inspection coordination and objective condition reporting to ensure contract specifications are respected.
  • Contract Negotiation Support Clear commercial terms, pricing mechanisms (FOB, C&F, Minimum Guaranteed Price), and payment alignment.
Operational Trade Support
Supply Chain Execution

Four-Stage Operational Trade Process

Nova Fresh International provides hands-on operational oversight from orchard sourcing through destination arrival to ensure product integrity and trade resolution.

01

Orchard & Grower Verification

Field-level evaluation of crop health, maturity indices, packhouse certifications (GLOBALG.A.P., PrimusGFS), and harvest timing.

02

Phytosanitary & Compliance

Ensuring export documentation, quarantine treatments, sanitary certificates, and customs paperwork meet destination regulatory requirements.

03

Cold Chain & Logistics

Monitoring temperature logs, container pre-cooling, ocean carrier bookings, and port handling protocols to preserve shelf life.

04

Arrival Inspection & Clearance

Coordinating destination port arrival inspections, condition reports, commercial reconciliation, and final settlement support.
