OUR SOLUTIONS & SERVICES

Market Development

(Chile, Japan)

Developing and executing market entry strategies, brand management, and market expansion strategies for Chilean and global produce exporters seeking access to Japan and key international markets since 2012. Our expertise covers grapes, cherries, blueberries, apples, kiwi, and citrus.

Produce Export Operations

(Japan, Chile)

NovaFresh International provides independent guidance on export representation, quality control, regulatory compliance, and market access. We work closely with growers, packers, exporters, and importers to manage trade, resolving quality disputes and establishing sustainable partnerships.

Produce Inspection & Ground Sourcing

(Japan)

Providing pre-shipment, arrival, and quality control inspection services for exporters into Japan. Our team inspects shipments upon arrival, ensures quality standards are met, and represents exporters during claims or inspection disputes.
