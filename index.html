Core Capabilities | Nova Fresh International

Nova Fresh International

Nova Fresh International helps companies identify international business opportunities, connect with the right partners, and develop new markets. We work with suppliers, producers, buyers, importers, and companies seeking to introduce products into new markets, providing sourcing, market development, coordination, and commercial support.

Market Development

  • Identify and develop new international market opportunities
  • Help companies enter or expand in specific target markets
  • Connect with verified commercial partners and buyer networks
  • Structure export and market-entry commercial programs

Sourcing & Partner Connections

  • Identify qualified producers, suppliers, and buyers worldwide
  • Match specific product specifications with verified suppliers
  • Provide buyers with multiple supply options and risk diversification
  • Coordinate specifications, pricing, and commercial contracts

Inspection & Quality Support

  • Pre-shipment and loading inspections at origin
  • Arrival quality verification at destination ports
  • Product specification compliance and reporting
  • Reconditioning & repacking coordination for commercial recovery
  • Commercial claim resolution and origin quality support

Markets, Products & International Trade Opportunities

Nova Fresh International sources products from South America, Central America, the United States, and the Caribbean, connecting qualified suppliers with buyers and developing international trade opportunities across these markets.

Our product and market opportunities include:

FRESH PRODUCE

Cherries, Blueberries, Avocados, Grapes, Citrus, Apples & Pears

INDIVIDUALLY QUICK FROZEN (IQF)

Avocado, Mango, Pineapple, Berries & Other Produce

FRUIT INGREDIENTS

Tropical Fruit, Purées & Concentrates

CACAO & SPECIALTY FOODS

Cacao, Cocoa, Chocolate & Coatings

Our product and market opportunities evolve according to the needs of our buyers, suppliers, and market development partners.

Milton A. Johnson

With more than 25 years of experience in international trade and food business, Milton A. Johnson has developed long-standing relationships with producers, suppliers, importers, and buyers across North America, Asia, Latin America, and the Caribbean.

His experience comes from working directly with companies and people involved in the international movement of food products—from the producer and packing house to the importer and final market.

Milton's approach is straightforward: build the right relationships, understand the market, and develop opportunities that can work commercially for all parties.
