Connecting Suppliers & Buyers Worldwide

Nova Fresh International develops international markets and connects qualified produce suppliers with buyers across North America, Latin America, Asia, and global markets.

Producers & Exporters

For Producers / Exporters

Unlocking access to lucrative international markets and establishing reliable, long-term commercial relationships.

Nova Fresh International helps producers and exporters:
  • Identify international opportunities — Access strategic growth markets in North America, Asia, and beyond.
  • Find qualified buyers — Connect directly with pre-screened importers, distributors, and retailers.
  • Develop market relationships — Build durable commercial trust and brand equity overseas.
  • Introduce products — Successfully launch fresh produce varieties tailored to local preferences.
  • Understand market requirements — Master phytosanitary standards, packaging specs, and regulations.
  • Establish sustainable business relationships — Create practical, long-term multi-season partnerships.
Importers & Buyers

For Importers / Buyers

Securing high-quality fresh produce supplies through transparent, direct supplier introductions and support.

Nova Fresh International helps buyers:
  • Identify qualified suppliers — Partner with vetted growers and exporters committed to export quality.
  • Source specific products — Acquire precise fresh produce categories, pack specs, and seasonal volumes.
  • Evaluate opportunities — Assess harvest forecasts, quality benchmarks, and market competitive dynamics.
  • Coordinate introductions — Facilitate direct, transparent communication with reliable growers.
  • Support negotiations — Navigate commercial terms, pricing structures, and contractual agreements smoothly.
  • Develop new supply relationships — Expand supply chains with dependable international agricultural partners.
TRADE & MARKET SUPPORT

Comprehensive Operational & Trade Services

Providing direct operational management, quality control, and strategic intelligence to support successful international fresh produce transactions.

Export Coordination

End-to-end guidance through international trade requirements, regulatory compliance, export documentation, and phytosanitary protocols for cross-border shipments.

Product / Quality Inspection

Independent pre-shipment and arrival quality assessments to ensure produce standards are strictly met, protecting both exporters and importers during claim resolution.

Ground Sourcing

Direct, on-the-ground crop evaluation, grower relationship management, and orchard-level procurement across premier production regions in Chile, North America, and beyond.

Market Intelligence

Real-time market analysis, pricing trends, seasonal volume forecasts, and competitive intelligence to empower informed commercial decisions for producers and buyers.

Logistics Coordination

Managing cold chain integrity, freight routing, shipping schedules, and port operations to maintain optimum product freshness from origin farm to destination market.
